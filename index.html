About

I'm an SRE / Cloud Engineer with a decade of experience at the intersection of operations and software engineering. I care about systems that stay up, changes that are boring to ship, and tooling that leaves the next engineer better off than I found them.

Currently at Upstash, where I look after Kubernetes clusters, cloud infrastructure across AWS and GCP, internal platform services, and the quiet plumbing that makes a product feel trustworthy.

Away from the terminal, being a dad is the best part of my life. When I get time to myself I usually spend it playing PC games or picking up my electric guitar — both are how I clear my head. I'm always curious about tech and pick things up wherever the good stuff shows up.
