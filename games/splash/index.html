Splash

Trucks, diggers, cranes & planes

Keys on the left move things on the left, keys on the right move things on the right. The top row of keys moves things in the sky; the bottom row moves the cars. Space sends the bus. Tapping works too.

Full screen on/off: hold Left Ctrl and press Enter. Camera mode watches for movement; the video stays on this device.
